# Prospective partner outreach email

Use only after the sender's institution and any required ethics/process review
allow initial contact. Personalize only with verified contact details. This
is an exploratory invitation, not an agreement or request for identifiable
farm records.

**Subject:** Exploratory discussion: FieldShift research-prototype pilot

Dear [verified name or role],

I am contacting you on behalf of [verified institution/team] to ask whether
your organization might be open to an initial discussion about a small
Bangladesh agricultural field-data pilot.

FieldShift is a research prototype exploring how environmental context,
agronomic information, and documented field history could support crop
rotation planning and adaptive farm-management research. It is not a
validated service or forecast. We cannot promise improved yields, income,
water use, soil health, recommendations, services, or funding.

At this stage, we are seeking only a conversation about feasibility and
appropriate safeguards. No partner, farmer, field, data access, or agreement
has been assumed. We will not request or accept farm records through this
email. Any collection would require appropriate institutional review,
partner permission, informed voluntary farmer consent, agreed data-use and
privacy terms, and a documented collection protocol before work begins.
Participation would be voluntary, and declining would have no effect on any
existing services or relationship.

If useful, we would welcome a short introductory meeting to learn whether the
research scope is relevant, what approvals and data-governance requirements
would apply, and whether a later jointly reviewed protocol is appropriate.
There is no obligation to proceed.

Please let me know if there is an appropriate contact or process for an
exploratory discussion. You may also decline or ask that we not follow up.
